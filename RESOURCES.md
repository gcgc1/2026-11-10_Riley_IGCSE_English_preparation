# 0500 First Language English Resources

Curated for the 2027–2029 syllabus. Everything here is either a Cambridge primary document or a source that quotes one accurately.

## Knowledge

### Primary — the authority

- [Syllabus: 0500, 2027–2029 (PDF)](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/718783-2027-2029-syllabus.pdf)
  The final authority on structure, assessment objectives and word limits. Use for: checking any claim about what a question asks. Note: the paper structure changed for 2027 — older guides describe a different paper.
- [Specimen Paper 1 + mark scheme, 2027 (PDF)](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/718838-2027-specimen-paper-1-mark-scheme.pdf)
  The single most useful document in this workspace. Every band descriptor for Paper 1, plus worked indicative answers. Use for: learning what each mark is actually awarded for.
- [Specimen Paper 2 + mark scheme, 2027 (PDF)](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/718839-2027-specimen-paper-2-mark-scheme.pdf)
  Band descriptors for directed writing (25 marks) and composition (16 + 24). Use for: all writing practice.
- [Specimen Paper 1 question paper](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/718842-2027-specimen-paper-1.pdf) · [insert](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/718835-2027-specimen-paper-1-insert.pdf)
- [Specimen Paper 2 question paper](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/718843-2027-specimen-paper-2.pdf) · [insert](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/718836-2027-specimen-paper-2-insert.pdf)
- [Past papers and examiner reports](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-igcse-english-first-language-0500/past-papers)
  Examiner reports are the highest-value free resource Cambridge publishes: they say, in the examiners' own words, what candidates got wrong. Use for: diagnosing recurring errors.
- [Grade thresholds, June 2025](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/741386-first-language-english-oral-endorsement-0500-june-2025-grade-threshold-table.pdf)
  Use for: knowing what a grade A costs in marks (about 67%).

### Secondary — teaching and practice

- [Cambridge IGCSE First Language English Coursebook, Marian Cox (Cambridge University Press)](https://www.cambridge.org/gb/education/subject/english/english-first-language/cambridge-igcse-first-language-english)
  The standard endorsed coursebook. **Check you buy the edition endorsed for examination from 2027** — earlier editions teach the old three-question Paper 1. Use for: structured units on descriptive, narrative and discursive writing.
- [Save My Exams — 0500 past papers, organised by paper](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/english-language/cie/past-papers/paper-1)
  Convenient index of question papers, inserts and mark schemes. Use for: finding papers quickly. Always mark against the Cambridge mark scheme, not the site's summary.

### Reading

- [`reference/reading.html`](reference/reading.html) — **the authority in this workspace** for the block books, page targets, the Sunday sentence task and the five-word vocabulary method. Check it before borrowing or buying anything.
- [Accelerated Reader Bookfinder](https://www.arbookfind.com/)
  Where the word counts for every block book are verified. Use for: checking a title is 20,000–32,000 words before it is added to the list.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/IGCSE](https://www.reddit.com/r/IGCSE/)
  Large, active, mostly students. Use for: comparing notes on grade boundaries, finding which past papers are hardest, sanity-checking a predicted grade. Treat advice on technique with suspicion; treat shared mark schemes as useful.
- [The Student Room — IGCSE forum](https://www.thestudentroom.co.uk/forumdisplay.php?f=65)
  UK-centric, long-running threads. Use for: exam-season logistics, revision strategy, and reading how strong candidates describe their own process.
- **A marker, if one can be found.** The highest-value "community" for this mission is a human who marks 0500 for a living — a Cambridge examiner, or a school teacher willing to mark two scripts a term. Worth asking about, even while self-studying.

## Gaps

- **No examiner report exists yet for the 2027 structure.** The first exams under the new papers are 2027. Until reports are published, the 2027 specimen mark schemes are the only authoritative statement of standards. Revisit this in late 2027.
- **No confirmed endorsed coursebook for 2027** has been verified in this workspace. Check the Cambridge endorsement list before buying.
- **No human marker identified.** AI marking against the real mark schemes is the current feedback loop; it is good but not a substitute for a trained examiner's judgement on borderline scripts.
