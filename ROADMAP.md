# ROADMAP — 0500 First Language English, grade A by March 2029

121 weeks. **One lesson every week, except every fourth week, which is a revision test.**

- **97 teaching lessons** + **24 revision tests** = 121 weeks.
- Week 1 begins 2026-11-06. Week 121 ends 2029-02-25.
- Decision: **March 2029 series**, 121 weeks. Caveat: the March series is **India only** — if the centre is outside India, the target falls back to June 2029 (134 weeks) and every filename changes.

---

## 1. Naming scheme

**Lessons** — `lessons/NNN-121-YYYY-MM-DD.html`
**Tests** — `tests/NNN-121-YYYY-MM-DD-test.html`

| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `NNN` | Week number, zero-padded to three digits. Week 001 starts 2026-11-06. |
| `121` | Total weeks in the course. |
| `YYYY-MM-DD` | The **Sunday** of that week. |

**Formula:** Sunday of week *n* = `2026-11-08 + 7 × (n − 1)` days.

There is **one number only: the week**. Week 005 is week 005 whether it holds a lesson or a test. Do not number lessons separately.

| Week | File |
|---|---|
| 1 | `lessons/001-121-2026-11-08.html` |
| 2 | `lessons/002-121-2026-11-15.html` |
| 3 | `lessons/003-121-2026-11-22.html` |
| 4 | `tests/004-121-2026-11-29-test.html` |
| 5 | `lessons/005-121-2026-12-06.html` |
| 121 | `lessons/121-121-2029-02-25.html` |

**Eyebrow format**

- Teaching week: `Week 005 (121) · Due: 2026-12-06 · Lesson · Paper 1`
- Test week: `Revision test 4 (24) · Week 016 · Due: 2027-02-21 · 20 minutes`

## 2. The four-week rhythm

| Week in block | What it is |
|---|---|
| 1 | Teach — a new lesson |
| 2 | Teach — a new lesson |
| 3 | Teach — a new lesson |
| **4** | **Revision test** — no new lesson |

The test is **cumulative and interleaved**: roughly **60% older material, 40% from the last block**. It is timed, marked against the real mark schemes, and scored in `REVISION-LOG.md`.

Tests run at weeks **4, 8, 12 … 96** — 24 of them. Phase 3 (100–121) is assessment-heavy already, so the mock cycle replaces the four-week rule there.

## 9. The weekly rhythm

| Day | Session | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Lesson** or **Test** | 35–50 min | The week's lesson and its practice task, or the full test |
| Tue | **Mark & repair** | 20 min | Read the strand-level marking; rewrite the weakest answer |
| Wed | **Retrieve** | 20 min | This week's quiz cold, five words from the vocabulary bank, plus one older lesson |
| Thu | **Apply** | 30 min | One real exam question of the current type, timed |
| Sat | **Write** | 45 min | 350–450 words, composition or directed. Underline three bank words |
| Sun | **Read** | 45 min | The block book — to the page target, or to the end of the chapter. Bring one sentence |

**Reading is one book per four-week block**, read on Sundays at 45 minutes a sitting. The book, the page target and the five-word vocabulary method are on `reference/reading.html`; every lesson's Sunday row names the book and the pages. A book over 32,000 words takes two blocks and says so.

On a test week, the Mon session is the test itself and the Tue session is marking it.

## 10. Build cadence

- Build in **batches of 8 weeks**, one batch ahead of need.
- Never pre-build Phase 3 beyond the fixed lessons.
- Every lesson ends in something the learner produces and the teacher marks.
- Update `PROGRESS.md` at the end of every build session.

## 11. Standing facts

- Grade A ≈ **107/160 ≈ 67% ≈ 54/80 per paper**.
- Paper 1: 2h, 80 marks, four 20-mark questions, 80% reading.
- Paper 2: 2h, 80 marks, Section A 40 + Section B 40, 80% writing.
- Syllabus is the **2027–2029** version. Resources written before 2027 describe a different exam.
- Local syllabus, specimen papers and mark schemes are in `sources/`.
