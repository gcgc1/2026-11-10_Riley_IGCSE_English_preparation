How to work through a week

  1. Monday — the lesson. Read it top to bottom before you write anything. If a section is unclear, ask before attempting the boxes; a misunderstood skill practised three times is three times harder to unlearn.
  2. Monday — the practice boxes. Press Copy answer after each one and paste it into this chat. The page saves nothing: if it is not pasted, it is gone.
  3. Tuesday — marking and repair. Read the strand-by-strand marking, then rewrite the weakest answer. The rewrite is where the mark is won.
  4. Wednesday — retrieval. The quiz cold, no scrolling back, then five words from your bank, from memory. Add three more bank words to Saturday's writing, underlined.
  5. Thursday — apply. One real exam question of the current type, timed.
  6. Saturday — write. 350–450 words. Sunday — read the block book, 45 minutes, and bring one sentence.
  7. After a test, record the score and the weakest question type in REVISION-LOG.md. That column, not the average, decides what the next repair lesson is.
