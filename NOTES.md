## Teaching preferences

- Wants to be marked against the **real Cambridge mark schemes**, not generic advice. Always cite the band descriptor or the specific mark scheme line.

## Reading and vocabulary

- **One book per four-week block**, Sunday, 45 minutes a sitting. Block 1 is *Once* (Morris Gleitzman, 30,820 words). Blocks 1–4 are modern YA realism; classics start at block 5, timed to the imagery and Q3(d) lessons. A book over 32,000 words takes two blocks.
- **One sentence every Sunday**, brought to Monday's session: name the exact word, say what it adds, say why the writer chose it. Marked against Paper 1 Table E, briefly, alongside the practice answers.
- **Five words a week** from the book, on four-field cards; keep 6 of every 20 at the end of a block. Three must appear in Saturday's writing, underlined. The method is on `reference/reading.html`; the record is `vocabulary/bank.md`.

## Working notes

- The 2027 syllabus restructured both papers. **Any resource written before 2027 describes a different exam.** Check every source against the 2027–2029 syllabus before using it.
- Grade A ≈ 107/160 ≈ 67% ≈ 54/80 per paper. Use this as the standing target in lessons.
- Paper 1 is 80% reading; Paper 2 is 80% writing. Plan practice accordingly.
- Keep lessons under ~15 minutes of learner time. The runway is long; consistency beats intensity.
