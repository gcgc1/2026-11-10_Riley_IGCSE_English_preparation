Cambridge IGCSE First Language English 0500 Papers 1 & 2 First exam 2027
The target

Grade A in June 2025 needed 107 of 160 weighted marks — about 67%. That is roughly 54 out of 80 on each paper. Grade A* needed 121 (76%). You do not need to be perfect. You need to be reliably good on every question.

Paper 1 — Reading

2 hours · 80 marks · 50% of the qualification. Four compulsory questions, 20 marks each, on three texts printed in an insert (about 1,400 words total). Spend about 15 minutes reading. Dictionaries are not allowed.

Paper 2 — Directed Writing and Composition

2 hours · 80 marks · 50% of the qualification. Two questions, one from each section. Section A is based on one or two texts of 550–650 words in an insert.

Where the marks actually sit

Writing is worth 25 + 40 = 65 of Paper 2's 80 marks. On Paper 1, writing is only 15 of 80. So Paper 1 is won by reading precisely; Paper 2 is won by writing well. They are different skills and need different practice.

The assessment objectives, in plain English

AO1 — Reading (50% of the qualification)
CodeOfficial wordingWhat it means in practice
R1Demonstrate understanding of explicit meaningsFind what the text literally says. Quote or state it.
R2Demonstrate understanding of implicit meanings and attitudesRead between the lines. What does the writer feel? What is implied but not stated?
R3Analyse, evaluate and develop facts, ideas and opinions, using appropriate support from the textTake an idea and push it further, with evidence. "This suggests… because…"
R4Demonstrate understanding of how writers achieve effects and influence readersExplain word choices — connotations, images, sound, structure — and the effect on the reader.
R5Select and use information for specific purposesPick the right details for the task, not all the details.
AO2 — Writing (50% of the qualification)
CodeOfficial wordingWhat it means in practice
W1Articulate experience and express what is thought, felt and imaginedContent: is there something real and interesting here?
W2Organise and structure facts, ideas and opinions for deliberate effectShape: paragraphs, sequencing, a deliberate opening and ending.
W3Use a range of vocabulary and sentence structures appropriate to contextRange: precise words, varied sentence lengths and forms.
W4Use language appropriate to purpose and to engage the audienceRegister: does it sound like a speech / article / story, aimed at this reader?
W5Make accurate use of spelling, punctuation and grammarAccuracy. At the top bands, errors must be rare.
