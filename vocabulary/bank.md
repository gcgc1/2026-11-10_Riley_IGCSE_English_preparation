# Vocabulary bank

Five words a week, taken from the block book. The method is on
[`reference/reading.html`](../reference/reading.html) — this file is only where the cards live.

Rules, in short:

- Copy the sentence **exactly** from the book.
- Meaning in **eight words or fewer**.
- Your own sentence is about **something else**.
- At the end of each four-week block, **keep 6 of the 20**.
