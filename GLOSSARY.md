# 0500 Glossary

The working language of this workspace. Terms are added only once they have been used correctly in a lesson or in practice.

## Exam architecture

**Strand**:
One separable idea inside a question or phrase, worth one mark. A 2-mark "own words" question has two strands.
_Avoid_: Point, part, bit

**Lifting**:
Copying words or phrases from the source text into an answer instead of re-expressing them. Penalised explicitly in the mark schemes.
_Avoid_: Copying, quoting (quoting is deliberate and cited; lifting is not)

**Band descriptor**:
The level-by-level wording in a mark scheme that defines what a given mark range looks like. Marks are placed by matching a response to a band, not by counting features.
_Avoid_: Rubric, criteria list

**Insert**:
The separate booklet containing the reading texts. You may annotate it; you must not write answers on it.

**Directed writing**:
Writing that is shaped by a source text and a specified form, audience and purpose — Paper 2 Question 1(b).
_Avoid_: Guided writing, transactional writing

**Composition**:
The free-standing descriptive or narrative piece in Paper 2 Section B, written from a title.
_Avoid_: Essay, creative writing

## Reading

**Block book**:
The novel read across one four-week block — 20,000–32,000 words, 45 minutes each Sunday. A book over 32,000 words takes two blocks.
_Avoid_: Reading book, set text

**Bank word**:
A word met in the block book, carded, and kept after the block's cull. Bank words are retrieved on Wednesdays and used in Saturday's writing.
_Avoid_: Vocabulary item, word list
