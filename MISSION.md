# Mission: Grade A in Cambridge IGCSE First Language English (0500), Papers 1 and 2

## Why

To sit the 0500 First Language English exam in 2029 and come out with a grade A — as a second-language speaker, studying outside a classroom. A grade A in a first-language English qualification is proof of real academic writing ability, and it opens the door to A Level English and to essay-based subjects at university.

## Success looks like

- Scores **54+/80 on Paper 1** and **54+/80 on Paper 2** under full timed conditions, consistently — not once.
- Answers every "in your own words" question without lifting, and can explain *why* a lifted answer scores nothing.
- Writes a 350–450 word composition in 50 minutes that lands in band 5 or 6 for both content/structure and style/accuracy.
- Analyses a writer's word choices (R4) with precision, not just by naming devices.
- Turns a 550–650 word source text into a 250–350 word directed piece in the right register, in 45 minutes.
- Reads a mark scheme and predicts their own mark within 2 marks before being told.

## Constraints

- **Year 9 now (November 2026).** Exam in 2029 — roughly 2.5 years, which is a long runway and should be used for real skill, not cramming.
- **4–6 hours per week.** Sustainable, not bursty.
- **One lesson a week, with a revision test every fourth week.** 97 lessons + 24 tests across 121 weeks. The tests are cumulative and timed, and their scores are logged.
- **Self-studying.** No teacher marking work. Feedback comes from AI marking against the real Cambridge mark schemes, plus self-marking.
- **English is a second language.** 0500 is designed for first-language candidates. Accuracy (W5) and lexical range (W3) will need deliberate, sustained work — this is the main risk to a grade A.
- **No dictionaries in the exam.** Vocabulary must be internalised, not looked up.
- **Route: Paper 1 + Paper 2.** Not the Component 3 coursework portfolio.

## Out of scope

- Component 3 (Coursework Portfolio) — not being taken.
- Component 4 (Speaking and Listening) — separately endorsed, does not affect the grade.
- The 9–1 version, 0990.
- English Literature.
- 0510/0511 English as a Second Language — a deliberate choice has been made to take 0500 instead.

## Exam series

**March 2029.** The course runs 121 weeks, from 2026-11-06 to 2029-02-25. (The original request said "April 2029"; there is no April series.)

**Caveat.** Cambridge runs the March series **in India only**. If the exam centre turns out to be outside India, the target must fall back to **June 2029** — 134 weeks — and every lesson filename changes from `NNN-121` to `NNN-134`.
